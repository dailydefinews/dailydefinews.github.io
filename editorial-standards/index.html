Editorial standards

What we cover

Onchain Report covers the Crypto industry as news: market structure, protocol changes, regulation, funding and security. Stories describe events and their context, not where prices are headed.

Sources

We work from primary material wherever it exists — company and project statements, regulatory documents, on-chain records — and from reputable reporting. Sources are linked in the text so readers can check them.

Editorial independence

What we cover and how we frame it is an editorial decision. Naming a company, protocol or asset in a story does not mean we endorse it.

Accuracy and corrections

We aim to get every detail right. If a factual error is found, we fix the story.

Not financial advice

Nothing we publish is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any asset. Crypto markets are volatile, and readers should make their own decisions and seek professional advice where needed.
