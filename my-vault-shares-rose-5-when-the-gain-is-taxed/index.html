My vault shares rose 5%: when the gain is taxed

How DeFi lending vault earnings are taxed: rewards count as income when you control them; share-price gains usually count when you redeem or sell your shares.

Onchain Report NewsroomNov 27, 2026 · 23:43 UTC#803d4b3 min read

In the usual US case, DeFi lending vault rewards are taxed as income when you receive or control them, while growth in a vault share’s price is generally taxed as a capital gain when you redeem or sell. The answer can change where depositing transfers beneficial ownership of your tokens, as it may in some UK cases. That split reflects how Morpho vaults build yield into share value, while separate rewards arrive as new property; the IRS explains the control rule in Rev. Rul. 2023-14.

When does a vault gain become taxable?

For a US depositor, share-price growth is generally taxed when the shares are redeemed or sold. Say you deposit 10,000 USDC for 10,000 shares at $1 each, then redeem them when each share is worth $1.05. You receive 10,500 USDC, so the $500 increase is a capital gain at redemption.

The basic calculation is: shares redeemed × (exit share price − entry share price). The gain builds up inside the vault’s share price, so it usually isn’t realized while you hold the shares. That also means a bad-debt loss that cuts the share price reduces your eventual gain. If the loss takes the value below your cost, it may instead leave you with a capital loss.

Depositing can itself trigger a gain where the transaction counts as a disposal. HMRC says a DeFi loan can be a disposal if beneficial ownership of the tokens passes to the platform. That depends on the arrangement’s terms and what the recipient can do with the tokens. So a UK depositor may owe tax on a gain in the deposited asset at the point of transfer, before withdrawing from the vault.

How do I report DeFi vault income on my tax return?

For a US return, treat reward tokens as income at their value when you gain control of them. That value becomes their cost basis—the amount used to measure a later gain or loss. If you claim MORPHO rewards, record their value on the claim date. Selling those tokens later is a separate taxable event: compare the sale value with the claim-date value.

Share redemptions or sales generally go through capital-gains reporting, including Form 8949. Rev. Proc. 2024-28 provides a safe harbor for allocating unused basis to wallets or accounts as of January 1, 2025; US basis rules also require wallet-by-wallet tracking from 2025. Keep the date, share count, and share price for every deposit and redemption, per wallet and chain.

Do I owe tax on DeFi rewards I haven’t claimed yet?

Usually, not until you receive or control the reward. A reward may count before you click “claim” if it is already credited and available for you to use. The key is when you gain dominion and control, not just when you choose to move the tokens.

How are my earnings from a DeFi lending vault taxed?

For US purposes, IRS Notice 2014-21 treats crypto as property. HMRC’s CRYPTO61000 guidance covers DeFi lending and staking, but the UK treatment can depend on whether the deposit is a disposal and whether the return is income. These are general rules, not tax advice. Keep each vault’s deposit and exit records together so you don’t mistake a change in share price for reward income.
