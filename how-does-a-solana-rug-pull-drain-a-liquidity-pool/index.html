How does a Solana rug pull drain a liquidity pool?

How do rug pulls work on Solana? A creator keeps control of liquidity or token permissions, draws in buyers, then drains SOL, mints supply or blocks sellers.

Onchain Report NewsroomNov 21, 2026 · 13:38 UTC#554f943 min read

A Solana rug pull happens when a creator keeps an on-chain lever, attracts buyers, then uses that control to take pool funds or stop holders from selling.

The three levers are LP tokens, mint authority and freeze authority. Each gives its holder a way to change what buyers can do after a token starts trading.

A creator can drain the pool in one transaction

Take this illustrative case: a creator mints a token and seeds a Raydium liquidity pool with SOL, while keeping the pool’s LP tokens. A liquidity pool holds tokens so people can trade them; LP tokens represent a claim on both sides of that pool.

Linked wallets buy early, and the price rises. Outside buyers then add more SOL to the pool as they buy the token. The creator still holds the LP tokens, which can redeem both the SOL and tokens held in the pool.

A rugcheck reads the authorities, LP status and holder concentration to flag these levers. The linked topic rugcheck good or bad adds background on how to think about those risk signals.

Before the pull, the pool has SOL and tokens for buyers to trade against. The creator can redeem the LP tokens in one transaction, taking both sides of the pool. The withdrawal can complete in a single block, with no on-chain warning beforehand.

Afterward, the pool’s SOL is gone, leaving holders with tokens but no liquidity to sell into. Their wallet balances may look the same; what changed is the pool backing those balances. That is why a displayed token balance can remain while a practical exit disappears.

The other levers target supply or transfers. A Solana Program Library (SPL) token mint has a mint authority field. If the creator retains that authority, they can mint fresh tokens and dump them into the market, adding supply while buyers are trading.

An SPL mint can also have a freeze authority. If retained, that authority can freeze token accounts and block transfers, which can stop holders from selling. Revoking either authority removes that specific lever, but a creator who still controls LP tokens can still pull liquidity.

What readers ask next

Can funds lost in a Solana rug pull be recovered?

Recovery is uncertain. A blockchain transaction can show where SOL went, but tracing funds does not itself return them. Keep transaction details and report suspected fraud to the relevant platform and authorities.

Are rug pulls illegal?

They can be prosecuted as fraud, depending on the evidence and jurisdiction. In the United States, federal prosecutors have charged alleged rug pulls as fraud; an indictment is an allegation, not a conviction. The Justice Department’s case announcement describes one such charge.

Can a token with revoked authorities still be rugged?

Yes. Revoking mint and freeze authorities removes those controls, but it does not remove LP tokens held by the creator. The pool can still be withdrawn if the creator controls them.
