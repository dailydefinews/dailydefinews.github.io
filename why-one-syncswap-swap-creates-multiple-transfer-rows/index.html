Why One SyncSwap Swap Creates Multiple Transfer Rows

Why does my swap show several token transfers on the explorer? Each row logs a step; filter for your address to find the trade’s net input and output.

Onchain Report NewsroomNov 25, 2026 · 12:46 UTC#1704753 min read

One swap can create several token-transfer rows because it moves tokens through contracts in separate steps. So when you check a trade on the zkSync Era block explorer, the rows show the transaction’s movements, not just the amount you sent and received.

SyncSwap uses pools and a Vault to handle trades, and ETH may be wrapped into WETH before it reaches a pool. The zksync defi topic adds context about the setting where SyncSwap operates; the transfer rows show how one trade moves through it.

An ETH-to-USDC swap moves through several steps

In an illustrative ETH-to-USDC swap, the explorer may show the following sequence:

  1. ETH leaves your wallet. On zkSync Era, ETH is represented through a system token contract at 0x000000000000000000000000000000000000800a. That means plain ETH movements can appear in the token-transfer list alongside ERC-20 token rows.
  2. ETH becomes WETH. The swap wraps ETH into WETH, an ERC-20 token that a pool can use. This creates a WETH transfer row.
  3. WETH enters the Vault. SyncSwap pool tokens are held by the Vault, so the WETH row may show the Vault as the recipient rather than a pool address.
  4. USDC goes to your wallet. The Vault sends out USDC, creating another row with your address as the recipient.

Your wallet started with ETH and ends with USDC, minus the ETH used for gas. Gas is the network fee for processing the transaction; look for it in the transaction’s fee details, since it may be displayed separately from token transfers. The intermediate WETH movements are steps in the same trade, not extra USDC or ETH received by you.

A routed swap can add more rows. If the route goes through an intermediate token, such as ETH to one token and then to USDC, each hop adds transfers into and out of the relevant pool. Filter the transfer list for rows where your address is the sender or recipient to spot your net input and output. Rows between contracts describe the route.

Quote differences and internal transactions

Why does my swap show a different amount than the quote?

A quote is an estimate; the completed swap records the amounts settled in the transaction. The final amount can vary with the pool’s price as the trade executes and with the route used. Compare the amount that left your wallet with the amount delivered to it, rather than adding every row together.

What is the 'internal transactions' tab on a block explorer?

The internal transactions tab shows calls and value movements that happen as contracts interact during a transaction. It can help explain ETH movements alongside ERC-20 rows. Etherscan and other explorers may group or label these details differently, so read the sender, recipient, token, and amount on each row.
