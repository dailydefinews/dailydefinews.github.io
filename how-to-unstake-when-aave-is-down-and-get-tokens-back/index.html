How to unstake when Aave is down and get tokens back

Aave app vs Etherscan for unstaking: wait while your window has days left; use a verified contract call if it closes within a day to redeem your stake.

Onchain Report NewsroomNov 28, 2026 · 01:37 UTC#6760623 min read

Use the Aave Interface if your unstake window has days left; use Etherscan if it closes within a day. The contract can process your exit while the front end is unavailable, and missing the window means restarting the cooldown.

Should I wait for the Aave app or use Etherscan?

Choose based on how much time remains in the window and how comfortable you are making a contract call. With more than a day left, waiting gives the interface time to return. With a day or less, act through Etherscan if you can verify the contract and transaction details.

This is an exit from Aave staking, so first identify which staking system holds your position. Legacy stkAAVE uses redeem(to, amount) during its unstake window. Umbrella stake tokens are ERC-4626 vaults, a standard that represents deposits as shares. Their redeem method takes shares; withdraw takes an asset amount. The Aave staking comparison matters here: the right call depends on whether your position is legacy stkAAVE or an Umbrella vault.

How do I redeem Umbrella shares on Etherscan?

For an Umbrella stake token, use these steps once the cooldown has ended and the unstake window is open:

  1. Get the staking contract address from Aave’s official docs or address book. Open that address on Etherscan and confirm the contract is verified. Check that the network is Ethereum if that is where your stake is held.
  2. Connect the wallet that holds the stake tokens, and make sure it has ETH for gas. Open the contract’s Write as Proxy tab and connect the wallet there. Proxied contracts are called through this tab.
  3. In the read functions, call balanceOf with your staking wallet address. Copy the exact share balance returned. ERC-4626 redemption takes shares, not the underlying asset amount, so enter this balance to redeem the full position and avoid leaving share dust behind.
  4. In the write functions, call redeem with that share amount and your address as receiver. Review the method and transaction in your wallet before confirming. For legacy stkAAVE, use its redeem(to, amount) method instead; do not enter an asset amount into Umbrella’s share field.
  5. After confirmation, check your wallet: the stake token balance should be zero and the underlying asset balance should have risen. That confirms the redemption reached your wallet.

Next, follow this checklist in order:

  • Check how much time remains in the unstake window.
  • Identify whether your position is legacy stkAAVE or an Umbrella stake token.
  • Wait for the interface if more than a day remains; otherwise verify the contract and submit the matching redeem call.
  • Confirm the underlying asset is in your wallet and the stake token balance is zero.
