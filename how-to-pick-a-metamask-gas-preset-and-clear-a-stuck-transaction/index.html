How to Pick a MetaMask Gas Preset and Clear a Stuck Transaction

MetaMask gas setting low vs market vs aggressive: use Market for most transactions, Low when you can wait and Aggressive when timing matters; learn how to speed up a stuck transaction.

Onchain Report NewsroomNov 28, 2026 · 05:59 UTC#3c5e573 min read

Use Market for most MetaMask transactions, Low when you can wait, and Aggressive when timing matters. The presets adjust the priority fee and maximum fee, while the network sets the base fee.

Choose a preset on the confirm screen

Market is the sensible starting point for a pending action in a dapp. It gives you MetaMask’s estimate for current conditions without choosing the lower or higher fee option yourself.

Under EIP-1559, the base fee is burned and the priority fee, or tip, goes to the block producer. You pay the base fee plus the tip, up to the transaction’s max fee. A preset can change the tip and max fee; it cannot set the network’s base fee.

A lifi bridge is a cross-chain bridge aggregator that finds routes for moving crypto between blockchains. Route fees and transfer time can vary; lifi bridge fees and transfer time explains those factors.

  1. Start with the action you want to take in the dapp. When MetaMask opens the confirm screen, find the fee estimate labeled Market.
  2. Tap Market and choose Low, Market or Aggressive. Low saves on the tip, but your transaction may wait through busy blocks. Market suits most transactions. Aggressive offers a higher tip for a better chance of faster inclusion, and can make sense for a lifi bridge approval you need to send before its quote expires.
  3. Read the estimated time and max fee before confirming. The estimate is not a guarantee of when the transaction will be included. The max fee is the per-unit cap, so you will not pay more than that cap for each unit of gas used.
  4. Confirm the transaction. MetaMask’s Activity tab will show whether it is pending or confirmed. If it confirms, the task is done.

  5. Speed up a pending Low transaction

    Use Speed up on the pending transaction in MetaMask if a Low fee leaves it waiting and you need to move on. Ethereum transactions from one account follow nonce order, a sequence number assigned to each transaction. A later transaction from that account cannot pass a pending earlier one, so sending a new transaction may leave both waiting.
  6. Open the pending transaction in Activity and tap Speed up. MetaMask resends it with the same nonce and a higher fee, giving it a better chance of being included sooner. Review the updated fee and confirm the speed-up.
  7. Return to Activity and check that the transaction is confirmed. If it is still pending, it has not cleared the queue yet; use Speed up on that transaction again if you still need it processed.
