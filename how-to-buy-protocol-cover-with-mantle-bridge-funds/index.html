How to Buy Protocol Cover With Mantle Bridge Funds

Mantle funds can support protocol cover, but the purchase may need to happen on Ethereum. Learn what to bridge, what cover protects, and what to check.

Onchain Report NewsroomOct 16, 2026 · 04:13 UTC#598f603 min read

To buy Nexus Mutual protocol cover using funds on Mantle, bridge the amount you need back to Ethereum, then pay for cover in a supported asset. The key point is that the network where you buy cover can differ from the network where your DeFi position sits. A listing’s terms say which positions and risks it protects.

Do I need to bridge funds back to Ethereum?

For Nexus Mutual, yes: its app takes the cover fee on Ethereum mainnet. It accepts ETH, USDC, cbBTC, or NXM, so check what you receive from the bridge and swap if needed. Keep enough ETH on Ethereum for transaction fees too. For a comparison of routes and trade-offs, see this guide to the mantle bridge. The route you choose affects the transfer process and the risks you take while moving funds.

Bridging money back does not move your Mantle position. You can still seek cover for a position on Mantle: Nexus Mutual says its listings generally cover positions on EVM-compatible chains, unless a listing’s terms make an exception. The bridge is for getting the payment asset to the buying network.

How do I choose the cover I need?

Find a listing for the protocol where your funds are deposited, then read its cover wording before paying. Protocol cover is tied to named risks and positions. It does not protect every asset in your wallet or every kind of loss.

  • Protocol: Check that the listing names the protocol you use.
  • Risk: Read which events count, such as a smart contract exploit, and which losses are excluded.
  • Amount: Set the amount you could lose, rather than the full value of a larger position.
  • Proof: Enter the wallet address or position details the listing asks for. These details can matter if you later make a claim.

Cover is discretionary, which means a claim is assessed under the product’s terms; payment is not automatic. Check the exclusions, claim window, and any deductible before you buy. The price can change with demand and available capacity, so review the quote before confirming.

What happens after I buy?

Choose the listing, amount, cover period, and payment asset in the app. Confirm the quoted fee and transaction on Ethereum. The cover is represented by an NFT in the buying wallet, while the covered position can remain on Mantle if the listing allows it.

Save the transaction and cover details. If a loss occurs, those records can help show which address and position were covered. The practical rule is simple: bridge only what you need to pay, verify the cover applies to your Mantle position, and keep the wording and proof requirements in view.
