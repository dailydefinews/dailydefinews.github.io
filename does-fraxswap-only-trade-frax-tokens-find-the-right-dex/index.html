Does Fraxswap Only Trade Frax Tokens? Find the Right DEX

Fraxswap can trade beyond Frax tokens when an ERC-20 pair exists and has liquidity; the Fraxswap Factory lets anyone create such a pair. Use Fraxswap for…

Onchain Report NewsroomNov 24, 2026 · 02:07 UTC#62d0262 min read

Fraxswap can trade beyond Frax tokens when an ERC-20 pair exists and has liquidity; the Fraxswap Factory lets anyone create such a pair. Use Fraxswap for your next swap if its pair has enough liquidity, because pool depth determines whether the quote suits your order.

The Frax-only impression has a clear cause: Frax uses the exchange for its ecosystem liquidity, and Frax-related pairs hold most of the liquidity there. That reflects where assets have been deposited, rather than a rule about which tokens may trade. Frax Finance’s overview describes permissionless pair creation and the embedded time-weighted market maker.

The Factory’s createPair function accepts two ERC-20 token addresses without requiring a Frax token. Someone can create a non-Frax pair and supply both assets; traders can then swap against those reserves. A pair’s existence alone says little about the trade you will get. As an order consumes more of a shallow pool, its output worsens.

When Does the Fraxswap Router Suit Your Trade?

The Router suits your trade when a path through its own pools has enough depth for your order. The router contract calculates swap amounts using pairs associated with its Factory. It cannot draw on liquidity sitting in Uniswap, Curve, or Balancer pools. A token that trades heavily elsewhere may therefore have no funded route here, or a route through thin pools that produces a poor quote.

Say you want to exchange a non-Frax token for another asset. Compare the output for the same input size across available DEXes, including routes through intermediate tokens. If Uniswap or Curve has the deeper pool, choose that venue for the swap. If the exchange’s Frax-related pair has adequate depth, it may give you the better result. The token’s eligibility matters less than the liquidity available to your particular order.

The other reason to consider this AMM is its TWAMM long-term order feature. It executes a large trade over time against a pair, a feature ordinary Uniswap V2 pools lack. Spreading execution can reduce the impact of placing the whole order at once, but it cannot make a thin pair deep. For a large trade, compare the pair’s liquidity before deciding whether a long-term order is useful.

Compare funded routes and quotes at your intended order size, then trade where the available liquidity best serves your token.
